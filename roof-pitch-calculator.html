How roof pitch is calculated

Pitch compares how far a roof rises with how far it runs sideways. Builders in the United States usually write it as rise over 12, so a 6:12 roof rises 6 units for every 12 units of run. This calculator uses these formulas:

Pitch (per 12) = rise ÷ run × 12 Slope (%) = rise ÷ run × 100 Angle = arctan(rise ÷ run) Surface multiplier = √(1 + (rise ÷ run)²)

Rise and run must use the same unit. Inches, feet or any other unit works, because only the ratio matters.

Worked example

A roof section rises 6 inches over a run of 12 inches.

  1. Pitch = 6 ÷ 12 × 12 = 6:12
  2. Slope = 6 ÷ 12 × 100 = 50%
  3. Angle = arctan(0.5) = 26.57°
  4. Surface multiplier = √(1 + 0.5²) = √1.25 = 1.118

Pitch is not always a whole number. If a roof rises 4 feet over a 10 foot run, the pitch is 4 ÷ 10 × 12 = 4.8:12, which is a slope of 40% and an angle of 21.8°.

What the surface multiplier is for

A sloped roof has more surface than the flat area under it. Multiply the flat area of a roof section by the surface multiplier to estimate the sloped area. The result does not include overhangs, valleys, dormers or waste, so treat it as a starting point and ask your roofing supplier how much to order.

Before you buy

  • Measure at the same spot twice and use the average.
  • On a hip or complex roof, measure each roof plane on its own.
  • Pitch decides which roofing materials and underlayment are allowed. Read the manufacturer’s instructions before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

What does a 6:12 roof pitch mean?

A 6:12 pitch means the roof rises 6 inches for every 12 inches it runs horizontally. That is a slope of 50% and an angle of about 26.57 degrees.

How do I measure rise and run?

From the attic, hold a level horizontally against the underside of a rafter. Mark 12 inches along the level, then measure straight up or down from that mark to the rafter. That measurement is the rise for a 12 inch run. Do not walk on a steep or wet roof without proper safety equipment.

What is the run on a gable roof?

On a simple, symmetrical gable roof, the run is half the width of the building, measured from the outside wall to a point directly below the ridge.

How do I convert pitch to degrees?

Divide the rise by 12, then take the arctangent of the result. Most phone calculators have an arctan or tan-1 button. For 6:12, arctan(0.5) is about 26.57 degrees.

What is the minimum pitch for asphalt shingles?

Shingle makers set their own minimum slopes. Many standard asphalt shingles are meant for slopes of 4:12 or more, and some allow lower slopes with special underlayment. Check the installation instructions for the product you plan to use and your local building code.
