How the amount is calculated

Mulch is sold in bags (measured in cubic feet) and in bulk (measured in cubic yards). The calculator converts your measurements to feet, finds the volume, and shows it both ways.

Area (sq ft) = length (ft) × width (ft) Volume (cu ft) = area × depth (ft) where depth in feet = inches ÷ 12 Volume (cu yd) = cubic feet ÷ 27 Bags = round up (cubic feet ÷ bag size in cubic feet)

Worked example

A garden bed 12 ft long and 8 ft wide, mulched 3 inches deep. The price of $35 per cubic yard is an example only.

  1. Area = 12 × 8 = 96 sq ft.
  2. Volume = 96 × (3 ÷ 12) = 24 cubic ft.
  3. Cubic yards = 24 ÷ 27 = 0.89 cubic yards.
  4. Bags of 2 cubic feet = round up (24 ÷ 2) = 12. Bags of 3 cubic feet = round up (24 ÷ 3) = 8.
  5. Bulk cost = 0.89 × $35 = $31.11 (the calculator uses the unrounded volume).

One cubic yard is the same as 13.5 bags of 2 cubic feet, or 9 bags of 3 cubic feet. In metric, one cubic meter covers 10 square meters at 10 cm deep, and it equals 20 bags of 50 liters.

Before you buy

  • Check how much old mulch is already there before you add more. A thick layer can hold too much water.
  • Order a little extra for top-ups. Mulch settles and fades over time.
  • Leave a gap around tree trunks and plant stems.

Frequently asked questions

How deep should mulch be?

Garden guides commonly suggest about 2 to 4 inches, depending on the plants and the mulch. Keep mulch a few inches away from tree trunks and plant stems.

How many bags of mulch are in a cubic yard?

A cubic yard is 27 cubic feet. That is 13.5 bags of 2 cubic feet or 9 bags of 3 cubic feet. Bag sizes vary by brand, so check the label.

Is bulk mulch cheaper than bags?

For large areas, bulk delivery is often cheaper per cubic foot, but delivery fees and minimum orders vary. Compare the total cost for your quantity.

How do I measure a curved bed?

Split the bed into rectangles, calculate each one, and add the results. A little extra covers the gaps and rounded edges.

Why are the bag counts rounded up?

You cannot buy part of a bag, so the calculator rounds up. The exact volume is shown too, so you can decide whether to buy one less.
