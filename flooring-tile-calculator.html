How the count works

The calculator divides the floor area by the area one piece takes up, including its grout line or gap, then adds your waste allowance. The result is rounded up to whole pieces. If you enter pieces per box, it also rounds up to whole boxes.

Floor area = room length × room width One piece = (tile length + gap) × (tile width + gap) Pieces to buy = round up (floor area ÷ one piece × (1 + waste ÷ 100)) Boxes = round up (pieces to buy ÷ pieces per box)

Worked example

A 12 ft by 10 ft room with 12 inch by 12 inch tiles, a 0.125 inch grout line, 10% waste and 10 tiles per box.

  1. Floor area = 12 × 10 = 120 sq ft.
  2. One piece = (12 + 0.125) × (12 + 0.125) ÷ 144 = 1.0209 sq ft.
  3. Pieces before waste = 120 ÷ 1.0209 = 117.5.
  4. With 10% waste = 117.5 × 1.10 = 129.3, rounded up to 130 pieces.
  5. Boxes = round up (130 ÷ 10) = 13 boxes.

Assumptions and limits

  • The room is a simple rectangle. For an L-shaped room, calculate each rectangle and add the results.
  • For plank flooring, enter the plank length and width and a gap of 0.
  • This is an area estimate. It does not plan the layout, so cuts at the walls are covered by the waste allowance.
  • Diagonal patterns, large tiles, and rooms with many corners usually need more waste.

Before you buy

  • Order all pieces from one batch. Shades can differ between batches.
  • Keep a few spare pieces for future repairs.
  • Check the box for the area it covers. That number can be easier to use than counting pieces.

Frequently asked questions

How much waste should I add for tile or flooring?

Many installers suggest about 10% for a simple straight layout and 15% or more for diagonal patterns or rooms with many corners. Your supplier or installer can tell you what is usual for your product.

How do I enter the grout line?

Enter the width of the grout line or gap between pieces. For tiles it is often around 1/8 inch (3 mm), but follow your tile maker\u2019s advice. Use 0 for planks that fit tightly together.

Can I use this for planks and laminate?

Yes. Enter the plank length and width, and set the gap to 0. Then add your waste allowance.

Why compare the area with the box instead of the pieces?

Some flooring is sold by the box with the covered area printed on it. Compare the floor area with waste shown in the result with the area on the box.

Should I keep spare tiles?

It is a good idea to keep a few spare pieces from the same batch for future repairs, because later batches can differ slightly in shade.
