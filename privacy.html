Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version

The calculators run in your web browser. The numbers you type are not sent to us and are not stored by this site. We do not ask for your name, email address or any account.

What this site collects

This site does not use accounts, forms that send data, or cookies of its own. If you choose Metric units, the site saves that one choice in your browser’s local storage so it can remember it next time. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Hosting logs

The company that hosts this site (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.) may automatically record technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. We do not control these logs. Please read the host’s own privacy policy to learn more.

Analytics and advertising

At the time of the last update, this site does not use analytics and does not show ads. If we add analytics or advertising in the future, such as Google AdSense, third parties like Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads and measure results. We will update this page before that happens and add any notices or choices that the law requires.

Children

This site is a general-audience tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? See the Contact page.
