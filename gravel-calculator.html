How the volume is calculated

Gravel is sold by the cubic yard, but you measure a space in feet and inches. The calculator converts everything to feet, finds the volume in cubic feet, then divides by 27 because one cubic yard holds 27 cubic feet (3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft).

Area (sq ft) = length (ft) × width (ft) Volume (cu ft) = area × depth (ft) where depth in feet = inches ÷ 12 Volume (cu yd) = cubic feet ÷ 27 Cost = cubic yards × price per cubic yard

Worked example

A path 20 ft long and 10 ft wide, covered with 3 inches of gravel. The price of $45 per cubic yard is an example only.

  1. Area = 20 × 10 = 200 sq ft.
  2. Depth = 3 ÷ 12 = 0.25 ft.
  3. Volume = 200 × 0.25 = 50 cubic ft.
  4. Cubic yards = 50 ÷ 27 = 1.85 cubic yards.
  5. Cost = 1.85 × $45 = $83.33 (the calculator uses the unrounded volume).

Before you buy

  • Measure the depth after the ground is prepared. Gravel on uneven ground takes more.
  • Ask whether the supplier sells by volume or by weight, and compare prices in the same unit.
  • Weed barrier fabric is a separate item. Its area is the same as the area you entered.

Frequently asked questions

How many tons are in a cubic yard of gravel?

It depends on the stone and how wet it is. Crushed stone and gravel often weigh roughly 1.3 to 1.5 tons per cubic yard, and sand and road base can be a little higher. The calculator uses rough ranges, so ask your supplier for the figure for your material and enter it for a better estimate.

How deep should gravel be?

It depends on the job. A decorative path needs less than a driveway or a base under pavers. Follow the plan for your project or ask your supplier.

Should I order extra?

Many people add about 5% to 10% for settling, uneven ground and spillage. The calculator shows the exact volume, so add your own margin.

How do I measure an irregular area?

Split it into rectangles, calculate each one, and add the cubic yards together. A little extra covers the gaps and rounded edges.

Can I enter feet and inches mixed together?

Enter a single number with one unit. For 5 feet 6 inches, enter 5.5 feet or 66 inches.
