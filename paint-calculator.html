How the amount is calculated

The calculator finds the area of the four walls, subtracts doors and windows, multiplies by the number of coats, and divides by how much area one gallon (or liter) of your paint covers.

Wall area = 2 × (length + width) × wall height Paintable area = wall area − (doors × 20 sq ft) − (windows × 15 sq ft) (+ ceiling area if chosen) Paint = paintable area × coats ÷ coverage of one gallon

Worked example

A room 14 ft by 12 ft with 8 ft walls, 1 door, 2 windows and 2 coats. The paint covers 350 sq ft per gallon, which is an example only. Read the real number on the can.

  1. Wall area = 2 × (14 + 12) × 8 = 416 sq ft.
  2. Doors and windows = 1 × 20 + 2 × 15 = 50 sq ft.
  3. Paintable area = 416 − 50 = 366 sq ft.
  4. 2 coats = 366 × 2 = 732 sq ft.
  5. Paint = 732 ÷ 350 = 2.09 gallons, rounded up to the next quarter gallon: 2.25 gallons.

If you also paint the ceiling (14 × 12 = 168 sq ft), the paintable area becomes 534 sq ft and the total is 3.25 gallons.

Assumptions and limits

  • One rectangular room with flat walls. Each door is counted as 20 sq ft and each window as 15 sq ft. Your real openings may differ.
  • Trim, baseboards, crown molding, closets, primer and a second color are not included.
  • Rough or porous walls, new drywall and big color changes can need more paint than the can says.

Before you buy

  • Measure from floor to ceiling, not just the height of the window.
  • Buy all the paint for a room at once, or mix the cans together so the color matches.
  • Check the can for coverage. Rough or textured walls soak up more paint.

Frequently asked questions

How much area does a gallon of paint cover?

Many interior paints cover about 350 to 400 square feet per gallon on smooth walls, which is roughly 8.5 to 10 square meters per liter. The can shows the real number for your paint.

Do I need two coats?

Often yes, especially when you change color or paint over a dark color. Some paints cover in one coat on a similar color. Ask at the paint store, and check the can.

Does this include trim and baseboards?

No. Trim, baseboards and doors are usually painted with a different paint. Measure those separately, or ask the store how much they need.

Why are doors and windows removed?

They are not painted with wall paint, so taking them out avoids over-buying. The calculator uses 20 sq ft for a door and 15 sq ft for a window. Your real sizes may differ.

Should I buy extra paint?

A small amount left over is useful for touch-ups. If the calculator says 2.25 gallons, a 2.5 gallon purchase is a sensible round-up. Ask the store about can sizes.
