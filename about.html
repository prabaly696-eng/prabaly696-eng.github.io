About this site

Measure Twice Calculators is a small set of free tools for people planning home and yard projects. The idea comes from an old carpenter’s saying: measure twice, cut once.

What you will find here

Each calculator does one job: roof pitch, deck boards, retaining wall blocks, gravel, mulch, fence posts, concrete, paint and flooring. Most can switch between US units (feet and inches) and metric units (meters and centimeters). Every page lists the formula it uses and a worked example, so you can follow the math and check it.

What these tools are not

They are planning aids, not professional advice. They do not check whether a deck, wall or fence is safe, and they do not know your local building rules. For anything structural, talk to your local building department or a qualified professional.

Who runs this site

Measure Twice is an independent project created to make home improvement planning easier. We provide simple, free online calculators to help people estimate materials, plan projects, and make more informed decisions.

Found a mistake?

If a result looks wrong, please contact us with the page name, the numbers you entered and the result you expected.
